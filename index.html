Partnership details

Each partnership starts with a practical question: is there a fit between your brand, the intended audience and the channels you can accept?

We propose a measured launch with agreed definitions and a review point, rather than promising volume before the work has been tested.

  • Qualifying acquisitions. Agree eligible markets, audience criteria and what counts as a valid acquisition, not just a registration.
  • Reporting we can reconcile. Define attribution, conversion events and adjustments so both sides can assess the same results.
  • Review before expansion. Set the pilot scope and the evidence needed to discuss a broader commercial relationship.

Operator accountability

Zero tolerance
for operator fraud.

Reporting integrity policy

We do not accept player shaving, deliberate removal of earned attribution, or withholding of earned CPA and revenue-share commissions.

Detecting operator fraud is an explicit reporting requirement. We require RedTrack postbacks, retained player and click-ID history, and API or backend access for later reconciliation. Operators must explain and document missing records, attribution changes and commission adjustments.

Confirmed reporting manipulation is incompatible with a RiseLeads partnership.

02 / Define the events

A registration is not the same as a qualifying acquisition.

We require operator postbacks to RedTrack to be configured and tested before launch for registrations, first-time and repeat deposits, deposit amounts and currency, CPA qualification and commission, and revenue-share commission updates. We also request any additional events the operator can provide.

Define permitted traffic sources, creative approvals, commercial terms and responsibilities. A bounded pilot should answer agreed questions before either side commits to expansion.
