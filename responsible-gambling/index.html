Responsible gambling / Independent support

Know when
to step away.

Gambling can cause financial and personal harm. It is not a way to earn income, repay debt or recover previous losses.

Not an emergency service

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency service.

Not gambling is a valid decision

Reading terms can help identify restrictions. It cannot remove the risk of losing money or the possibility of harm. Only adults who meet the applicable local legal age and law should consider gambling. This website does not determine your eligibility.

Reasons to pause

Chasing losses, borrowing to gamble, hiding activity or gambling with money needed for essentials are reasons to stop and seek support. Time-outs, financial limits, blocking tools and self-exclusion may be useful, but none is a guarantee of safety.

Independent support in Great Britain

GamCare provides gambling-harm support and links to blocking, self-exclusion and money-management resources. Its published National Gambling Helpline is 0808 8020 133, available 24/7. This is a Great Britain resource, not a worldwide service guarantee.

If you are elsewhere, seek an appropriate local health service, regulator or gambling-harm support organisation. Services, eligibility and telephone charges can vary by location.

Before any decision

The Gambling Commission’s guidance for Great Britain explains checking licence details, domain names and terms. A licence check does not make a business risk-free or establish lawful availability in a different country.

What RiseLeads does here

This website does not accept bets, manage player accounts, recommend a bonus or provide treatment. Our partnership contact is not a gambling-support helpline. The editorial content is intended to explain questions and evidence limits, not encourage gambling.
