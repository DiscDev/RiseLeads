The proposition

A shared plan.
Agreed roles.

RiseLeads is a publishing and acquisition business focused on direct operator partnerships. Our proposed approach brings market-specific editorial content, agreed acquisition channels and performance reporting into the same partnership plan.

For an operator, the starting point is brand fit: intended markets, eligible audiences, permitted products and placements. For both sides, the next step is agreeing what will be measured and how a pilot will be reviewed.

We welcome CPA, hybrid CPA and revenue share discussions. Qualification rules, attribution windows, deductions, negative carryover, payment timing, commission duration and termination belong in the written agreement. Specific rates and review thresholds would be discussed privately.

Operator accountability

Zero tolerance
for operator fraud.

We do not accept player shaving, deliberate removal of earned attribution, or withholding of earned CPA and revenue-share commissions.

Our reporting requirements are designed to detect operator fraud. We require stable links between the RedTrack click ID, the operator's pseudonymous player reference and commission records, with retained postback history and API or backend access for later reconciliation.

Previously reported players or click IDs that disappear from equivalent reports, unexplained reattribution and retrospective commission changes must have a documented explanation. Legitimate corrections must remain traceable. The integration plan must define access, retention and how discrepancies will be investigated.

Confirmed reporting manipulation is incompatible with a RiseLeads partnership. We expect discrepancies to be resolved with supporting records and any earned commission found to be due to be corrected under the agreement.

Measurement & reconciliation

Agree what success
means before launch.

A registration, first-time deposit and qualifying acquisition are different events. We would agree how each is attributed, reported and reconciled, along with the net gaming revenue definition and adjustments where revenue share applies.

Operator postbacks required before launch

We require server-to-server postbacks from the operator to RedTrack to be configured and tested before launch for:

  • Completed registrations.
  • Deposits, including first-time and repeat deposits, with deposit amounts and currency.
  • CPA qualification and commission amounts.
  • Revenue-share commission updates.

We also request any additional events the operator can provide, such as KYC status, withdrawals, refunds, chargebacks, reversals and net gaming revenue updates, using only the fields agreed for attribution and reporting.

Deposit amounts are not affiliate earnings. CPA qualification and revenue-share commission updates must follow the agreed commercial definitions, including deductions and later adjustments.

Before launch, we agree the RedTrack click ID (our click reference), event mapping, unique event ID, event time, currency, validation, retries, duplicate handling and reversals, then validate attribution and amounts in RedTrack with authorized test events. Where supported and approved, API access or scheduled reports can supplement postbacks. Any unavailable events or reporting gaps must be resolved in the integration plan before launch.

The review should cover attribution windows, reporting delays, duplicate events, adjustments and a process for resolving discrepancies. Data access would be limited to the fields needed for an agreed purpose, with access and retention terms settled privately.

Separate review

From first discussion to review

Start with a pilot.
Decide what comes next.

  1. Confirm the fit

    Identify brands, lawful target markets, languages, products and permitted channels. Confirm age and audience restrictions rather than inferring eligibility from a translated page.

  2. Agree the commercial definitions

    Set qualification rules, commission structure, attribution and payment terms. Agree what evidence would support a commercial review.

  3. Validate the reporting

    Review available postbacks, APIs or reports. Where supported, use authorized test events to check attribution and reconciliation before live activity.

  4. Approve a bounded pilot

    Agree placements, creative claims, responsibilities and limits. Obtain the required sign-offs before launch; operator acceptance does not replace platform requirements.

  5. Review before expanding

    Assess acquisition quality and agreed measures together. Resolve discrepancies and discuss expansion or revised terms only after reviewing the evidence.

Practical partnership questions

Which markets and languages are you considering?

Market and language scope would be agreed per brand. Country eligibility, licensing and permitted acquisition channels need verification before launch; no country availability is represented by this website.

Can we see examples of the publishing approach?

Yes. The casino terms guide and Spinny Casino methodology demonstration show how sources, unknowns and terms can be presented. They are editorial examples, not campaign results or firsthand casino tests.

Are the proposed integrations already connected?

This site presents a proposed partnership approach. It does not demonstrate a connected operator integration, active campaign, verified audience or signed partnership. Technical capability and permissions would be assessed for the specific arrangement.

What should we share first?

Your brand or programme URL, permitted markets and languages, channel restrictions and a business contact are enough for an initial discussion. Do not send player data, credentials or private account material.
