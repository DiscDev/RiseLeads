What this site does not collect

The authored site has no newsletter, SMS or account forms, audience uploads, analytics, advertising pixels, affiliate tracking or simulated submissions. There are no third-party font or media embeds. We do not claim that a consent preference or subscriber relationship has been created.

Motion preference

If you use the motion control, the site stores only your on/off choice in your browser’s local storage under riseleads-motion. It is not sent by this code to RiseLeads. You can change it using the control or clear the site’s browser storage. Your device’s reduced-motion preference is always respected; the video is not requested while that preference is active.

Ordinary web delivery

Your browser requests the pages and local assets needed to display them. A hosting provider may process technical request data such as IP address, browser information and access time for delivery or security. This notice does not promise that the hosting provider keeps no logs or performs no service-level processing. Technical request handling and retention are governed by the hosting provider’s applicable practices and terms, not by the absence of authored analytics.

Email and external resources

To contact us, type the address shown as an image into your email application. Sending email is your separate action, handled by your provider and the recipient’s email service. Any information you choose to send may be used to respond to your enquiry. Avoid sending sensitive personal information, player data or credentials.

External source links lead to independently operated websites with their own privacy practices. They are not embedded here. The public partner brief downloads directly from this site without a registration form.

Scope and changes

This notice describes the current website, not every future service. New services or changes to data handling require a review of applicable legal requirements and an updated notice before they are enabled. No registered-entity status is asserted by this notice.
