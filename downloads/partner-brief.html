The proposition

RiseLeads is a publishing and acquisition business for online casino operators. We seek direct partnerships built around useful market-specific content, agreed traffic sources and clear performance reporting.

What we would agree with operators

  • Market fit. Brands, eligible audiences, countries, languages and permitted products and placements.
  • Qualifying acquisitions. Definitions for registrations, first-time deposits, qualified CPA events and invalid-traffic exclusions.
  • Reconciled reporting. Attribution, reporting periods, adjustments and a process for resolving discrepancies.
  • A review point. A bounded pilot with agreed responsibilities and criteria for discussing expansion.

Publishing and proposed channels

Original educational guides and source-backed brand research form the proposed publishing approach. Paid search, social, video and native placements would be considered case by case, with operator and advertising-platform approvals confirmed before launch. Email or SMS activity would require a separate review of consent, permitted content, unsubscribe handling and suppression.

Zero tolerance for operator fraud

We do not accept player shaving, deliberate removal of earned attribution, or withholding of earned CPA and revenue-share commissions.

Detecting operator fraud is an explicit reporting requirement. We require stable RedTrack click-ID and pseudonymous player attribution, retained postback history, and API or backend access for later reconciliation. Previously reported records that disappear, unexplained attribution removals and retrospective commission changes must have a documented explanation. Legitimate corrections must remain traceable.

Confirmed reporting manipulation is incompatible with a RiseLeads partnership. Access, retention, supporting records and the process for resolving discrepancies and correcting earned commission must be agreed with the operator.

Measurement and technical fit

We require operator server-to-server postbacks to RedTrack to be configured and tested before launch for registrations, first-time and repeat deposits, deposit amounts and currency, CPA qualification and commission, and revenue-share commission updates. We also request any additional events the operator can provide, including KYC status, withdrawals, refunds, chargebacks, reversals and net gaming revenue updates, within the agreed reporting scope.

Deposit amounts are not affiliate earnings. CPA qualification, commission values, net gaming revenue and revenue-share adjustments must match the commercial agreement. Before launch, we agree the RedTrack click ID (our click reference), event mapping, unique event ID, event time, currency, validation, retries, duplicate handling and reversals, then validate attribution and amounts in RedTrack with authorized test events.

Where supported and approved, API access or scheduled reports can supplement postbacks. Any unavailable events or reporting gaps must be resolved in the integration plan before launch. This brief describes integration requirements, not an already connected operator system.

Data sharing would be limited to the agreed purpose and minimum necessary fields. Access, retention and reporting responsibilities would be settled privately.

Commercial discussion

CPA, hybrid CPA and revenue share arrangements can be discussed. Qualification rules, attribution windows, deductions, negative carryover, payment timing, commission duration and termination require a written agreement. Rates and review thresholds would be discussed privately; no performance or earnings guarantees are offered.

Proposed launch process

  1. Confirm brand, market, language and channel fit.
  2. Agree commercial terms and qualifying-event definitions.
  3. Validate tracking and reporting using authorized test events where supported.
  4. Approve placements, creative claims, responsibilities and a bounded pilot.
  5. Review acquisition quality and agreed measures before discussing expansion.
